# Beetles

> For: anyone building or slicing beetle work. Lets them: know which beetle
> rules are settled. Open questions live on the beetles pitch card in Trello.

Settled so far (from the beetles pitch and the card overhaul):

- **Pockets are beetles.** A beetle is the pocket; there's no separate
  pocket visual.
- **Inputs on the wings.** The gems a beetle needs appear on its wings.
- **Outputs on the body.** What it pays out appears on its body, under the
  wings.
- **Wings open to reveal outputs**, both when the player hovers the beetle
  and when the beetle receives a gem.
- **Growth is a scale change.** A beetle grows by scaling up.
- **Cards place beetles.** A beetle card places or replaces a beetle in a
  slot. A card is a beetle scene plus a reward config.
- **Slots are fixed for now:** three pre-set slots along the bottom of the
  board. Dynamic placement is a later bet.
