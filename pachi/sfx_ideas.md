## 💡 Audio Juice & Godot Engine Implementation Tips

1. **Consecutive Pin Hit Pitch Escalation**:
   - Instead of purely random pitch variation on pin hits, increment pitch along a musical scale (e.g., Pentatonic or Major scale) for consecutive pin hits within a short time window (resetting after 0.5s of silence). This transforms chaotic bouncing into procedural melody!
2. **Velocity-Based Impact Modulation**:
   - As implemented in [`Ball.cs`](file:///C:/Users/Axel/workspace/axelmagn/pachi/pachi/src/balls/Ball.cs#L287-L296), dynamically scale `VolumeDb` and `PitchScale` based on impact speed to convey kinetic weight.
3. **Polyphony & Voice Capping**:
   - Multi-ball situations can trigger dozens of pin collisions simultaneously. Ensure `AudioStreamPlayer2D` or your audio bus uses a limiter/compressor and max polyphony cap to prevent harsh audio distortion or clipping.
