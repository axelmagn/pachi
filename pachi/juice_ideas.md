## 🥇 Priority 1: Core Physical & Audio Feedback (Highest ROI)

Immediate feedback improvements for primary player interactions.

### 1. Pin Hit Juice (Visual Pulse, Sparks & Pitch-Escalating Audio)
* **Context:** Balls currently trigger velocity-based bounce audio in [Ball.cs](file:///C:/Users/Axel/workspace/axelmagn/pachi/pachi/src/balls/Ball.cs#L245-L261), but pins lack visual reaction when struck.
* **Implementation Plan:**
  * **Visual Pulse:** On hit, perform a fast 0.08s scale bump (`1.0` $\rightarrow$ `1.25` $\rightarrow$ `1.0`) via `Tween` on the hit pin and flash its `SelfModulate` to white.
  * **Hit Particles:** Spawn 3–5 tiny spark particles (`GPUParticles2D`) pointing outward along the impact normal.
  * **Pitch Escalation:** Replace random pitch variation with scale-based pitch modulation—each consecutive pin hit within a short window increments pitch up a musical scale degree (e.g. pentatonic scale), resetting after a brief pause.

### 2. Screen Shake & Micro-Hitstop System
* **Context:** Hits, launches, and pocket payouts currently occur without camera response.
* **Implementation Plan:**
  * **Screen Shake Manager:** Add a `Camera2D` screen shake system listening to events from [GlobalEvents.cs](file:///C:/Users/Axel/workspace/axelmagn/pachi/pachi/src/main_game/GlobalEvents.cs). Trigger directional impulses on heavy ball launches, pin impacts, and pocket rewards.
  * **Micro-Hitstop (Frame Freeze):** Momentarily pause physics processing for 1–2 frames (`GetTree().Paused = true`) on major jackpot pocket completions or rare ball collisions for maximum impact punch.

### 3. Launcher Charge Tension & Release Mechanics
* **Context:** [Launcher.cs](file:///C:/Users/Axel/workspace/axelmagn/pachi/pachi/src/launcher/Launcher.cs) rotates linearly during charge and features TODOs for charge curves and audio cues.
* **Implementation Plan:**
  * **Visual Charge Bar / Arc:** Render a dynamic charge meter overlay that changes color (green $\rightarrow$ yellow $\rightarrow$ pulsing red) as charge ratio increases.
  * **Plunger Squash & Stretch:** Compress/squash the launcher arm sprite while charging to communicate kinetic tension.
  * **Recoil Snap & Launch SFX:** Upon release, snap the launcher back with elastic easing (`Tween.EaseType.Out` / `TransType.Elastic`), trigger a punchy spring release sound, and apply a downward screen impulse.
