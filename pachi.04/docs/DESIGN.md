# Pachi Design

An incremental pachinko game where you buy balls and upgrade the board with 
cards.

## Game Phases

1. **Play:** The player launches balls from the hopper into the board.  The
   balls fall through pegs and into jackpots, or the bottom sink.

2. **Shop:** The player buys cards and booster packs which can be used to add
   balls to the hopper, upgrade board components, or change rules of play.

## Incremental Mechanics Brainstorming

- Balls accumulate bonus & mult as they bounce, similar to balatro

- Tech tree is implicit - as new nodes are unlocked, they become cards in the
  shop to be bought.

- Instead of just straight up buying cards - booster packs allow you to pick 
  the type of card and then choose the result.
